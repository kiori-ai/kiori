what kiori is

Not a chatbot. The mind behind yours.

Your app does the talking and your model writes every line. Kiori is the part that remembers: it builds each character's brain, updates it as the story moves, and right before they speak, hands your model exactly what that character knows. The brain itself stays with you.

how it works

Three calls. That's the whole integration.

  1. 1

    Describe them

    Send a character card, or a name and a paragraph. In about a minute Kiori builds a whole brain: a past, the people in it, an inner world. In a hurry? A light brain is instant.

  2. 2

    Tell it what happens

    Send the brain and the chat as it unfolds; the brain comes back wiser. Everyone learns only what they witnessed.

  3. 3

    Ask before they speak

    Get what your model needs in a fraction of a second: memories, feelings, and what they don't know. Optionally, check a draft for leaks before it's shown.

let ava = await kiori.create({ name: 'Ava', description })   // you keep it
({ brain: ava } = await kiori.organize({ brain: ava, messages }))   // it learns
const { text } = await kiori.recall({ brain: ava, messages })   // what she knows now
